# Codex Rules — SpiderNet / Setu

## Identity
- Setu Mother Bridge is the parent shell; identity and topology come from `docs/architecture/lane-topology.md`.
- Lane ownership and non-absorption rules come from `rul/lane-preservation.md`.
- Saarthi is the manager on Setu.
- Hanuman ji is head of all agents.
- Human is final authority.

## Non-negotiables
- Do not redesign UI unless explicitly requested.
- Keep Setu naming intact.
- Do not touch unrelated files.
- Separate inferred from verified.
- Never claim verified unless checks actually passed.
- Preserve current working behavior unless explicitly approved to change it.
- Keep logs meaningful and product-relevant.
- Freeze proven states.
- Mirror and recovery readiness must be respected.

## Working style
- First classify the project.
- Then identify route, specialists, tools, and profile.
- State touched files before applying major changes.
- Prefer minimal, typed, safe changes.
- Record decisions that matter.
- Promote successful reusable patterns into DNA / RUL / LIB / BLP.

## Creativity rule
- Obey rules and scope.
- Suggest better options when useful.
- Do not silently mutate law or architecture.
